## <span class="lang lang-zh">單線圖</span><span class="lang lang-en">Single Line Drawing</span>

<div class="lang lang-zh">
單線圖以及物件導向的設計操作，建立了抽象的設計思維與真實世界間的函數關係，是一種無論在建築業界還是原始村落，都可以有效溝通的語言。
</div>
<div class="lang lang-en">
An efficient tool for design and communication which forms an association between the abstract lines and the concrete world, that can be both effective for the professionals and the extremely underdeveloped.
</div>

## <span class="lang lang-zh">1. 向傳統工匠學習</span><span class="lang lang-en">Learning From Traditional Chinese Carpenters</span>

<div class="lang lang-zh">
傳統木匠施工時，並沒有工業製材可直接取用，因此他們會以原木兩端為基凖以吊墨線的方式，為自然有曲度的原木建立一條虛擬的中心線，再以此中心線放樣加工成建築的組件，而常民強化輕鋼系統便是以「單線圖」為基礎來進行設計與生產施工。
</div>
<div class="lang lang-en">
At the time when industrial straighten lumber was not available, traditional carpenters would create a center line on the crooked lumber by using the ink marker (Chinese version of a chalk line). They would then assemble and build based on this virtual center line.
</div>

## <span class="lang lang-zh">2. 單線圖的應用</span><span class="lang lang-en">Application of The Single Line Drawing</span>

<div class="lang lang-zh">
單線圖的概念及其應用，在不同的歷史背景下及專業領域內都可找到它的脈絡。從傳統木造營建體系，工匠師傅使用的單線圖及蓋房過程中搭配的口訣和詞彙，到現代化專業分工下，房屋結構強度的設計計算與評估，都是使用類似的簡單線條搭配文字以減少溝通的信息量。
</div>
<div class="lang lang-en">
The concept of the single line drawing and its application can be found throughout the history of construction. From ancient Chinese timber structures, to modern day structural design and engineering. These virtual lines with simple notes were, and are being used to reduce the information in order to simplify the communication.
</div>

## <span class="lang lang-zh">3. 參數的載體</span><span class="lang lang-en">Carrier of Parameters</span>

<div class="lang lang-zh">
常民強化輕鋼系統單線圖中單一線條的表達，可同時攜帶多種含義及參數，並依據不同的使用目的轉換。如：在方案階段用來定義房屋型式，再依據結構評估調整長寬高尺寸、鋼材斷面規格強度等；生產階段用來明確料件長度及連接固定位置及方式；施工階段用來提供構造材料安裝的參照點，同時更能作為工程整體成本管控的基礎。
</div>
<div class="lang lang-en">
In the Reinforced Lightweight Steel Framing system, lines within the single line drawing can simultaneously carry a set of definitions, which can be transformed for different application purposes. From the adjustment of dimensions to building forms; the specifications of steels for structural analysis; and the production management to construction.
</div>

## <span class="lang lang-zh">4. 有效的溝通語言</span><span class="lang lang-en">An Effective Language</span>

<div class="lang lang-zh">
相對於現行營建體系下細緻並且繁複的建築圖紙，單線圖更加精準也更具普適性，能夠跨越不同群體、產業層級及專業領域，在彼此間形成一種有效的溝通語言。同時，在此語言覆蓋的群體範圍內，形成高效率的溝通，使得營建過程中能夠更好的進行相互整合、協作並且有效地將經驗累積、傳承，並在此基礎上加以創新。
</div>
<div class="lang lang-en">
In comparison to the current elaborate and overly-complexed construction drawings, single line drawings have the advantage to better facilitate communication across different disciplines and industries. Based on the language of single line drawing, the process of design and construction can work more cooperatively, and experience can be integrated and accumulated more systematically for further optimization or application.
</div>
