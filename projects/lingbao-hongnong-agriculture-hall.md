  ### Background


  Hongnong Hall is a non-governmental organization established by Professor Huili He. The project is expected to provide a space for various usage to the local community in order to foster interaction among villagers.


  ### Program and Function


  To allow certain flexibility for multiple use, the project consists of a main hall with large sheltered open space, and a urine-feces diverted public toilet. The main Hall functions as reading house that is open to public with amenities such as kitchen, exhibition space, meeting rooms and meditation room for locals. The hall also provides temporary accommodation during large-scale cultural events in the village.   


  ### Site Context


  Located in Loess Plateau, the landscape of Lingbo is formed by rivers and terraced lands. Traditional earth dwellings named “Yao-dong” or “house cave”, which are

  built by carving out the hillside or horizontal excavation from a sunken courtyard, are commonly seen around the area. Harsh winter with heavy snow and cold wind are the major climatic features for residents to tackle.


  ### Strategical Response


  *Use of local materials*


  Local soil is a commonly used material for insulation and thermal mass to mediate temperature difference between building interior and exterior. Therefore, in this project, thick earth wall is applied to absorb the heat coming from the traditional “Kang”.


  *“Kang” - utilizing excess heat*


  “Kang” is a bed stove shapes as a long platform that channels the hot air exhausted from the cooking fireplace to outside. By using “Kang”, excess heat run across the

  brick wall barriers, forming hear current that provides comfortable and warm living platform for users. 


  *Daylighting*


  With an east-facing orientation, the staggered two-way pitched roof as well as the horizontal strip windows at three sides allow adequate incoming sunlight in the morning, providing warmth to the interior. In addition, the interplay of sunlight and pine trees create vivid changes of light and shadow to the interior spaces.

  ### 建造緣由


  弘農書院是中國人民大學人文學院副院長何慧麗教授發起的非營利性民間社團組織，書院致力於弘揚中華文化，探索生態文明背景下農村的可持續發展之路。弘農堂設計之初考慮作為書院對外接待的公共建築，使用機能範圍涵括廚房、餐廳、茶室、會議室、展覽空間、村民紅白喜事治理空間、禪修空間，亦可應對舉辦大型活動時舖位緊張的住宿問題。


  ### 地理位置


  靈寶由河川、土塬、山地台階式組成。地勢南高北低，自北向南，海拔從黃河的308 米到小秦嶺的2413 米，氣候是大陸性半濕潤季風型，氣候溫和，四季分明，平均氣溫14.5 度，年降雨量606.5 毫米。年日照2253 小時。


  ### 建築特色


  *靈活應用原材料*


  基地採挖出來的泥土以鋼網包覆出厚土牆，以土壤蓄熱的特性儲存日照所釋出的熱能，令晚上的室內溫度不會隨室外氣溫變化而大幅下跌。


  *使用傳統「火炕」取暖*


  由於北方冬天相當漫長，為了抵禦嚴寒，火炕是中國北方被廣泛應用的傳統取暖工具。火炕的灶口與廚房相連，使燒飯時釋放出來的熱空氣能再被使用。火炕內部的間牆以磚建成，使暖流能覆蓋火炕。炕上蓋上石板，成為多用途空間，更可於炕鋪之上擱置炕桌，對應木窗外西山景緻，是很好的茶歇空間。屋頂設置磚作煙囪，以利煙道通暢。


  *採光系統*


  屋頂採用錯層雙坡頂型式，由於建築坐西朝東，上午時分陽光透過門前一排松樹撒進來，室內光影斑駁，加上建築大空間三面橫向通長開窗，周圍地勢開闊，樹木山頭掩映，全天都可獲得較好的明暗感受，以及光線變化在空間內帶來的運動感、時間感。
