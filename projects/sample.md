body_en: >
  ### Project Overview


  This project is situated within a complex urban fabric where modernity constantly negotiates with deep-rooted traditions. The building stands not only as a physical structure but also as a mediator of eras—reflecting the shifts in cultural identity, lifestyle, and community needs. The site was once home to a row of early 20th-century shophouses. Instead of erasing the past, the design strategy reinterprets the scale and rhythm of the original urban grain. This generated a layered spatial sequence that transitions from the street into a semi-public courtyard, then into private interiors, emphasizing a porous relationship between inside and outside.


  ### Spatial Organization


  The spatial layout centers around a vertical and horizontal axis that enables light and air to permeate deep into the structure. The central courtyard acts as a living lung, facilitating ventilation and passive cooling, while also functioning as a communal heart. On the upper floors, living and working areas are distributed in a modular grid that allows future adaptation—responding to the evolving demographics and working patterns of its inhabitants.


  A key challenge was to resolve privacy without compromising openness. The design adopted operable louvers and vegetated buffers, granting users control over transparency and light. Staircases are strategically located not only as circulation cores but as spatial connectors, giving access to multiple semi-public terraces and view corridors.


  ### Materials and Sustainability


  The material palette draws from regional vernacular: timber for warmth and human scale, brick for texture and craft, concrete for durability, and glass for lightness and permeability. These materials were chosen not only for performance but also to tell a story of cultural and climatic specificity. Sustainability was addressed through passive strategies—solar orientation, cross-ventilation, rainwater harvesting, and roof gardens—minimizing mechanical dependency.


  In terms of construction, the project favored local labor and materials to support the regional economy and reduce embodied carbon. Modular units were preassembled to reduce on-site waste. Recycled bricks from the old shophouses were reused in the new façade, preserving a tactile memory of the site.


  ### Community Interface


  At the street level, the building opens into a porous ground plane that hosts a neighborhood café, exhibition space, and multi-purpose room. These spaces are intentionally left unprogrammed to allow spontaneous use—from pop-up markets to reading clubs—acting as a catalyst for informal exchange.


  Residents participate in the maintenance and curation of the shared areas, reinforcing a collective sense of ownership. The result is a dynamic urban organism that adapts and grows with its people, offering both shelter and belonging.


  ### Reflection


  The project serves as a prototype for regenerative urban development—sensitive to history, adaptable to the future, and grounded in the present. It demonstrates that architecture is not merely about building form, but about cultivating relationships—between people, between timeframes, and between the built and natural environment.

body_zh: >
  ### 計畫簡介


  本案基地位於一個歷史紋理與當代需求交織的市中心。它不只是單純的新建築，而是一種回應時代演變的設計實踐。設計初衷並非徹底抹除舊有脈絡，而是將過去的空間尺度、街道肌理和社會活動重新詮釋，將歷史轉化為當代表達的材料。基地原為一排老式騎樓房屋，設計保留其建築比例與步行節奏，透過分層空間邏輯打造由街道進入半戶外中庭、再通往私人空間的轉譯過程，強調開放與包容的場域。


  ### 空間組織


  整體空間圍繞中軸線展開，設置中央光井與通風廊道，使自然光與風得以深入各樓層。中庭不僅提供通風功能，更作為居民之間交流的平台。樓層配置強調彈性，透過模組化的格局設計，能因應不同家庭結構或工作形態進行調整。為了達成「開放中有隱私」，設計加入可調光遮簾與植栽牆面，讓住戶能自由調節遮蔽與視覺穿透。


  樓梯設計也跳脫單純交通功能，轉化為社交平台與景觀節點。多處平台與露台創造出不同尺度的聚會空間，同時打開視野，建立與都市環境的連結。


  ### 材質與永續策略


  建材選擇反映地方語彙與氣候條件，包含原木、手工磚、清水混凝土與穿孔金屬板，形成溫潤與理性並存的語言。舊有騎樓建築所拆除的磚材被回收並融入新立面中，為建築注入時間記憶。設計也導入自然通風、雨水回收系統與綠化屋頂，減少機械依賴，提高能源效率。


  營建過程強調在地協作，盡可能採用本地建材與工法，不僅減碳，也活絡地方經濟。模組預製元件則加快施工效率並降低廢料產生。


  ### 社區參與


  建築地面層以半開放形式與社區對話，包含咖啡廳、多功能展演空間與公共閱覽室，彈性場域讓居民能自發舉辦活動，從二手書市集到週末講座，豐富社區生活樣貌。住戶也共同參與中庭與屋頂的維護與佈置，使公共空間成為社群凝聚的平台。


  此模式成功示範建築不僅能創造空間，也能創造社會連結與文化再生的可能。


  ### 設計反思


  本案不只是空間形構，更是都市生活方式與社區經營的新提案。透過歷史的再詮釋與環境友善設計的整合，嘗試為未來都市居住型態提供一個可延伸、可複製的雛型，也讓建築真正成為社會改變的一部分。
